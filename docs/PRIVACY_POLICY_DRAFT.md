# Little Words Privacy Policy — publication draft

**Drafted 30 September 2026. Before publishing, replace the contact placeholder below with the actual publisher's privacy contact, publish this policy at a stable public web address, and confirm it still matches the final release build.**

Little Words is an offline reading practice app for children and their families. A parent or caregiver can create a reading profile, choose practice settings, and record how a child reads words and short sentences.

## Information used by the app

The app stores the name entered for each reading profile. It also stores each profile's practice and appearance settings, cards shown, reading attempts and scores, reading time, session dates and progress milestones. A profile name can be a child's name; families can choose a nickname instead. The app uses this information to show the selected child's progress, resume practice, and choose future practice items.

This information is stored in the app's private storage on the device. Little Words does not require an account. It does not ask for a child's email address, phone number, location, photos, contacts, or microphone access. Reading is judged by the accompanying adult; the app does not record speech or listen to the child.

## Information sent or shared

The current app has no internet permission or service connection. It does not send profile names, reading results, or other app data to the publisher or third parties. It has no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDK. The publisher does not receive a copy of the reading history through the app.

## Storage, retention, and deletion

The app stores data in Android's app-private storage. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for the app's data. Data stays on the device until the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared in Android settings. Either action removes the local profiles, settings, and reading history. There is currently no in-app export, restore, or individual profile deletion feature. A normal app update may preserve local data when Android accepts the update.

Because the app does not receive the local data, the publisher cannot view, correct, or erase it remotely. A parent or caregiver can remove all of it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Please be aware that this also removes progress for every profile on that device.

## Children and changes

Little Words is intended for parent-guided early reading practice. Parents and caregivers control what profile name they enter and how they record each reading attempt. If a future version changes what information the app uses or sends, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
